# 0xAgent Unified Icon Generation Prompts

## 🎨 Master Art Direction & Aesthetic Guidelines

- **Primary Palette**: Pitch-Black OLED (`#09090b`), Deep Obsidian Graphite (`#121216`), Pure Platinum White (`#f4f4f5`), Ghost Silver (`#a1a1aa`).
- **Accent Lighting**: Subtle cold electroluminescent rim light, frosted diffusion, zero rainbow/neon gradients.
- **Core Motif**: The cybernetic "0x" monogram — precision-milled titanium/obsidian ring (`0`) interlocked with a tactical diagonal cross (`x`), containing an optical sensor / sentient agent core at center with subtle horizontal scanline/glitch displacement.
- **Style**: Tactile brutalism, industrial sci-fi, ultra-minimalist vector-like 3D render with matte ceramic and frosted glass surfaces. No cartoonish elements, zero emoji tropes.
